# Modern Control Map - Nexus page draft

## Short description (350 characters max)

A complete modern controller layout for The Witcher 3: attacks on the triggers and bumpers, a button of its own for
each sign, tap-to-dodge and hold-to-run on B, both swords on X, and every context - on foot, in combat, on horseback,
swimming and sailing - laid out to match. A one-click installer sets it and leaves your keyboard alone.

## Description (BBCode)

[size=5][b]Modern Control Map[/b][/size]
A complete controller layout for The Witcher 3, built the way modern action games play: attacks on the triggers and
bumpers, a button of its own for each sign, and every context laid out to match.

[quote][b]Required game settings[/b]
The map is made to be played with these options:
[list]
[*][b]Options > Gameplay > Quick Sign Casting: Quick Casting[/b] - required. Holding LT and pressing a sign's button
casts that sign only in Quick Casting. [i]The installer sets it.[/i]
[*][b]Options > Controls > [Controller Only] Sprint by pressing Left Stick: Off[/b] - required. While it is on, the game
ignores B for running on a controller, and the map gives the left stick to Witcher Senses, Roach and the quick item.
[i]The installer turns it off.[/i]
[*][b]Options > Accessibility > Automatically Apply Blade Oils: On[/b] - required. The map has no oil buttons (the D-pad
holds are potions), so without it oils go on only from the inventory. [i]Set it yourself in the game's options.[/i]
[*][b]Options > Accessibility > Manual Sword Drawing/Sheathing: Off[/b] - recommended. With it off the game draws the
right sword for the fight by itself; X still draws either sword whenever you want. [i]Set it yourself.[/i]
[/list][/quote]

[size=4][b]The layout[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Exploration[/b] - LT cast sign · hold L-Stick Witcher Senses, press it twice to call Roach · D-pad Up quick item ·
RT strong attack · RB fast attack · Y jump and climb · X swords · B sprint (tap to toggle, hold to run) · A interact
[*][b]Combat[/b] - LT cast sign · LB parry · L-Stick quick item · RT strong attack · RB fast attack · X swords · B tap to
dodge, hold to run · A roll
[*][b]Horseback[/b] - LT cast sign · D-pad Up Witcher Senses · RT attack · RB crossbow · Y jump · X swords · B canter and
gallop · hold A to dismount
[*][b]Swimming and diving[/b] - D-pad Up Witcher Senses · L-Stick crossbow · B dive and swim fast · Y surface and climb
out
[*][b]Sailing[/b] - A speed up · X slow down · B disembark · RB crossbow · D-pad Up Witcher Senses
[*][b]Everywhere[/b] - D-pad Left and Right potions (hold to swap) · D-pad Down quick access menu · R-Stick camera lock
and quest tracking
[*][b]Sign casting[/b] - hold LT, then RT Igni · B Quen · Y Axii · X Yrden · RB Aard
[/list]
The images show every button in every context.

[size=4][b]What it adds beyond the bindings[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Sprint[/b] - out of combat a tap of B switches running on or off, and a hold runs while held.
[*][b]Swords[/b] - X draws or sheathes the steel sword on a tap and the silver sword on a hold, switching when the
other one is out.
[*][b]Dismount[/b] - the hold-to-dismount circle follows A. The game always put it on B, where Gallop now is.
[*][b]Signs[/b] - with LT held, each sign has its own button; on their own those buttons do their normal job, and LT
alone still casts your selected sign.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installing[/b][/size]
Install the archive with a mod manager. Close the game, then run [i]Install Modern Control Map.bat[/i] once. It sets
the controller bindings, leaves every keyboard binding as it is, and backs up each file it changes. [i]Uninstall Modern
Control Map.bat[/i] puts them back. Patch 5.0 (Remastered). No other mod required.
The map is set by the installer and is not meant to be changed from the Controller Scheme page.
